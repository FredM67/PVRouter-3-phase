The targets of the scheduled boosts whose window is open.

No boost outside the off-peak period, nor when the temperature is above the threshold. A missing or failed reading (DEVICE_DISCONNECTED_RAW, -7040) counts as cold on purpose: the boost runs, and the water heater's own thermostat stays the safety limit; the threshold only stops the boost before the heater's setting.

Parameters
bOffPeaktrue during the off-peak period
elapsedms since the start of the off-peak period (millis() - its start)
temperature_x100the temperature of the probe checked against the threshold, x100
threshold_x100the temperature above which no boost runs, x100
countthe number of boosts
readBoostcallable taking a boost index (below count), returning its DualTariff::Boost
Returns
the union of the masks of every boost in its window
