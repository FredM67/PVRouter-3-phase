The mains cycles from the conversion to the first read.

750 ms rounded up to whole cycles, plus one: a transaction may start up to a little after its cycle began (39 cycles at 50 Hz, 780 ms; 46 at 60 Hz, 767 ms).

bool TempSchedule::fitsInPeriod ( const uint8_t noOfSensors,
const uint8_t supplyFrequency,
const uint16_t periodCycles )
constexpr

Whether a round ends within its datalog period.

The round starts at the first cycle of the period, or the next one if the first is late; one cycle per second of the round may be late; and it must be over before the last cycle of the period, whose datalog copies run in the ADC interrupt at L1's crossing, before the new cycle is flagged.
