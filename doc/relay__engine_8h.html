Detailed Description

Decisions of the relay diversion: which relay switches, and when.

Author
Frédéric Metrich (frede.nosp@m.ric..nosp@m.metri.nosp@m.ch@l.nosp@m.ive.f.nosp@m.r)
Version
0.1
Date
2026-11-21

The relay engine without its I/O: the thresholds, the minimum ON and OFF times, the settle time after any relay change, the order the relays come in and go out, and the EWMA average they decide on. On each change, the relay calls the switched() of its Output type, which drives the pin in the firmware (utils_relay.h). Its native tests are in test/native/test_relay_engine.

Note
This header has no Arduino dependency (its whole include closure is plain C++).
