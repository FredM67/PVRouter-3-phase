Detailed Description

The data of the IoT frame, for TeleInfo::writeFrameStep().

The frame is written a line per step, over the next passes of the main loop: about 300 characters at 9600 baud 7E1, 0.3 s, well within the datalog period (1 s at least). Each value must be that of the datalog period of the frame until its last step:

  • tx_data, the Shared::copyOf_... copies and the relays' average only change at the end of a datalog period (the ISR writes the copies once per period, the main loop tx_data and the average once per datalog event);
  • the relay states, the tariff and the absence of diverted energy count change every second: they are taken by snapshot() at the datalog event, when the buffered frame took them.
