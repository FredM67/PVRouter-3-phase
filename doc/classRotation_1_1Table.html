Detailed Description

template<uint8_t NG, uint8_t NL>
class Rotation::Table< NG, NL >

The rotation groups, resolved: the priority places each group holds.

A group's places are the positions of the priority list that hold its loads at startup, in ascending order. rotate() moves the load in a group's last place to its first place and the others one place down, as LoadPriorities::rotate() does for the whole list; loads in no group never move.
