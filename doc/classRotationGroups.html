Detailed Description

template<uint8_t N, uint8_t MaxPins = 16>
class RotationGroups< N, MaxPins >

The rotation groups, as in config.h: the loads that take turns at the top of the priority list, one group per appliance (the elements of a 3-phase water heater).

Each group is a list of target codes, as for the override pins: LOAD(n), LOCAL_LOAD(n), REMOTE_LOAD(n), ALL_LOCAL_LOADS()... The codes stay codes here; Rotation::Table (rotation_groups.h) holds the priority places each group rotates in. One constructor takes up to 10 groups: each group needs at least 2 loads, and the router drives at most 12 local and 8 remote loads, so no valid config needs more.

NNumber of groups.
MaxPinsMaximum number of targets per group.
