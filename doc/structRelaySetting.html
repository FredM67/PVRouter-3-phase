Detailed Description

The settings of one relay, as in config.h: { pin, surplus, import, minON, minOFF }.

Thresholds in W, minimum times in minutes. Left out, they take the defaults of RelayLogic::Relay: 1000 W, 200 W, 5 and 5 minutes.
