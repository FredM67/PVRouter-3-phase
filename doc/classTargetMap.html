Detailed Description

template<uint8_t NL, uint8_t NR, uint8_t D>
class TargetMap< NL, NR, D >

What the target codes of config.h stand for, once the load map and the relays are known.

LOAD(n) is the override pin of load n (overridePinOf()), LOCAL_LOAD(n) its local pin, RELAY(n) the pin of relay n, and ALL_...() every pin of their kind. Out of range, or RELAY() without relay diversion, a code gives pin 0, which no load or relay uses: validation.h reports it. Any other value is a pin already.

Template Parameters
NLNumber of loads in the load map.
NRNumber of relays.
DDuration of the sliding average of the relays (unused here).
