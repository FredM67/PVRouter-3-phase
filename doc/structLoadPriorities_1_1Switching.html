The decision of one mains cycle: may switch one load ON or OFF.

Not inlined on purpose, as updatePhysicalLoadStates() in processing.h: inlined, it grew the ISR by 76 bytes, with 4 more registers to save and a stack frame on every call. Called once per mains cycle, it costs a call and a return there. Grid simulator: average 0.1 cycle less, one-off max 2 to 8 cycles more; with remote loads, 32 cycles less on average and 77 less at most.
